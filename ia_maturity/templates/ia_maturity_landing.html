Questions

Frequently asked

Who should complete the assessment?

Usually the Chief Audit Executive. Audit Committee members and executives can also respond – each gets their own assessment, so perspectives can be compared.

Can I stop and come back later?

Yes. Answers are saved as you go. Use “Continue / view results” with the same work e-mail to pick up where you left off.

Who sees our answers?

Your answers are confidential to you and IRSAA Business Solutions. Other organisations only ever see anonymous, aggregated benchmarks.

Why do you need my work e-mail?

To confirm you represent the organisation being assessed and to keep your results private to you. We send a one-time code – there is no password to remember.
